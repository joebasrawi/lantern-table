# Lantern Table

An open-source browser RPG for friends who play together or at different times. AI, human, and assisted DMs share one persistent rules engine. The approved visual direction is a minimal illustrated scene, quiet navigation, and on-demand character details.

## Product commitments
- Private campaigns, authenticated membership, invite links, saved state.
- Configurable pace, turn deadlines, consent-based absence actions, party votes.
- Quickplay for beginners; a detailed original tactical ruleset for experienced players. Do not claim full D&D/5e compatibility until an SRD implementation is complete.
- Any era or custom world; campaign constraints and consistent mechanics.
- Preset, guided, and custom character concepts with inspectable numbers.
- Adventure, Journal, Map, character and party-chat panels, human DM desk.
- AI narrates engine outcomes, never directly edits authoritative character resources.
- Minimal 2D artwork and fast immediate non-AI actions.
- Open source with a portable hosting path. The private game is deployed on Railway with independent password accounts. Verified-email registration and recovery are implemented but await a sender and live delivery validation before public onboarding.

## Milestones
1. Playable campaign loop: join, character, exploration, human/AI DM, combat, saved return.
2. Harden collaborative flows, privacy, stale actions, idempotency, and verified deployment.
3. Complete character art upload/generation, party scheduling options, richer rule content and independent hosting.
4. Public multiplayer release: billing controls, account lifecycle, notifications, comprehensive multi-device browser validation.

Completion of the first milestone does not complete the whole product. Unimplemented controls must not pretend to work.
