# Artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool for this project, then converted lossily to WebP for delivery without changing composition. These are original generated images, not official D&D artwork.

- `public/art/harbor.webp`: 1672×941, approximately 122 KB.
- `public/art/portraits.webp`: 1254×1254, approximately 142 KB, four portraits in a 2×2 atlas.

## Harbor prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: standalone background illustration for a simple immersive 2D browser fantasy RPG, not a website mockup. Wide cinematic hand-painted 2D rainy harbor tavern exterior at dusk. Landscape 16:9 composition. A weathered coastal tavern facing a rain-slick cobbled quay, distant sailboats and quiet teal sea, soft mist. Elegant understated fantasy illustration, visible painterly brushwork, atmospheric depth, warm amber lanterns and windows contrasting deep desaturated teal shadows and sea. Tavern on the right, harbor and open dark moody sky on the left. No text, UI, sign lettering, logos, borders, prominent characters or 3D rendering.

## Portrait prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Square character portrait atlas for a simple 2D fantasy browser RPG. Exact 2×2 grid of equally sized square head-and-shoulders portraits touching edge to edge, no gutters or borders. Top left: adult female human ranger with auburn hair, green hooded cloak and leather armor. Top right: adult female dark-skinned human rogue with black hair and dark leather armor. Bottom left: adult male scholarly human mage with wavy greying hair and understated dark blue robes. Bottom right: adult male dwarf warrior with reddish beard and practical weathered steel armor. Dark charcoal backgrounds, coherent hand-painted fantasy illustration, soft amber rim light and subdued teal shadows. No text, UI, logos, watermark, decorative frame or 3D rendering.
